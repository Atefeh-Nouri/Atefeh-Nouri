## 🧠 About Me

Master's student in **Neurocognitive Psychology** at Carl von Ossietzky University of Oldenburg (Germany) with a rigorous quantitative foundation (B.Sc. GPA: 18.95/20). Currently serving as a **Student Assistant (HiWi) at the Ambulatory Assessment Lab**. 

My research profile centers on **cognitive ergonomics, diagnostic decision architectures, executive function assessment, and neurocognitive testing methodologies**. I am experienced in structured task analysis, psychometric experiment administration, ambulatory assessment workflows (REDCap), and open-source collaborative research bridging human cognitive modeling with data-driven workflows.

**Current Themes:** Cognitive Modeling, Electrophysiology (EEG/ERP), Mobile Surveys & Ambulatory Assessment, and Psychometric Pipeline Design.
